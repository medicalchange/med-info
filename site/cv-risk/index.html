Icosapent ethyl eligibility

Icosapent ethyl (IPE) is a prescription purified EPA omega-3 medication used as an add-on to statin therapy in selected patients.

See diabetes-pathway qualifying CV risk factors
CCS diabetes-pathway qualifying factors: men ≥55 years or women ≥65 years, smoking now or within 3 months, hypertension or BP medication, low HDL-C, hsCRP >3.0 mg/L, eGFR 30 to 59, retinopathy, micro- or macroalbuminuria, or ABI <0.9 without claudication.
