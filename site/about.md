## What this site is

MedicalLinks is a collection of clinical references, decision-support tools and patient handouts for primary care and palliative care in Ontario. It is maintained by a practising physician as a convenience for clinicians, learners and patients. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any hospital, clinic, health authority or publisher whose material is linked here.

## Not medical advice

- **For patients:** The information here is general and may not apply to you. It does not replace advice from your own doctor, nurse practitioner or pharmacist. In an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.
- **For clinicians:** The tools and summaries support, but do not replace, clinical judgement. Check doses, thresholds and recommendations against the original guideline or product monograph before acting on them.

## Keeping information current

- Most pages show the date they were last updated. Linked guidelines and handouts belong to their publishers, who may revise or withdraw them; check the original source for the newest version.
- Some documents are older but are kept because they remain useful. The year is shown in the title where it matters.
- The drug shortage tool shows a filtered nightly snapshot from Health Product Shortages Canada (only active reports with an estimated end date), and may lag behind the official database. Check the displayed refresh time; a delayed or failed refresh can leave an older snapshot.

## Privacy

- Do not enter patient names, health card numbers or other identifying information into any tool on this site.
- The site uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), a privacy-focused page counter that does not use tracking cookies, to see which pages are used. There is no advertising.
