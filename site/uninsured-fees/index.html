    Coverage and other payers

    Do not charge patients for insured services or their administrative components. Routine record sharing for patient care is not a chart-transfer fee.

    • Accessible parking / transit and CAS forms for a child: excluded from patient billing. MOH forms may be unremunerated or have a specific OHIP code.
    • Immunization and TB testing: check the reason for the request. School or required student placement, LTC admission and medical indications can be insured. An unfunded vaccine does not automatically make its administration uninsured.
    • WSIB forms: use the applicable WSIB payment arrangements; the WSIB FAF is billed to WSIB.

    CPP disability reports
