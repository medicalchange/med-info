Where can I get my test done?

Bloodwork and imaging near 840 Coxwell Avenue, Toronto

Blood test or ECG

If you have a requisition for blood or urine tests, you can take it to a community laboratory that offers your test and accepts your form. LifeLabs and Dynacare have locations nearby.

  • Bring your requisition and OHIP card each time you go. If the form was sent electronically, confirm that the laboratory has it.
  • Check the laboratory’s website for current hours and appointment options before visiting, particularly around holidays.
  • For an ECG or a special test, check that your chosen location offers it and whether you need an appointment.
  • Fasting or other preparation depends on the test ordered. Follow your test instructions and ask the laboratory if you are unsure.

Imaging test

For an X-ray, ultrasound, mammogram or bone density test, choose an imaging centre that offers the test on your requisition. The locations below are nearby options.

  • Bring your requisition and OHIP card. If your form was sent electronically, confirm that the imaging centre has received it.
  • Book ultrasound, mammography and bone density tests in advance. The X-ray services below accept walk-ins, but their X-ray hours may differ from their regular opening hours.
  • Follow any preparation instructions from the imaging centre. Some tests require fasting or other preparation.
  • If you have had the same test before, tell the imaging centre where it was done so they can arrange comparison with earlier images when needed.

For CT, MRI or a test arranged at a particular hospital, follow the booking instructions from your referring clinician or the facility. These tests usually need an arranged appointment.

Requisitions and results

What if I do not have a printer?

Ask the testing location how to send your requisition electronically before your visit. Showing the form on your phone may not be enough. See the LifeLabs requisition instructions or select “Upload a Requisition” on the Dynacare website. Allow the provider’s stated processing time. Uploading a requisition does not book an appointment.

Will there be a charge?

Many medically necessary tests are covered by OHIP, but some tests or services have a fee. Confirm coverage with the provider before testing, particularly if you do not have a valid OHIP card.

How do I get my results?

Check that your referring clinician’s details are on the requisition so the report goes to the right person. Ask your clinician how and when to follow up.

Can I get a copy of my images?

Contact the imaging centre directly for a copy of the actual images. Your clinician may receive a written report rather than the images themselves.
