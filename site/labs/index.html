Common questions

Do I need to fast?

Fasting depends on the test ordered. Follow the instructions on your requisition and those provided by the laboratory or imaging centre. If the instructions are unclear, ask before your appointment. Ultrasound and other imaging tests may have their own preparation requirements.

What if I do not have a printer?

Ask the testing location how to send your requisition electronically before you visit. Do not assume that showing the form on your phone will be enough.

  • LifeLabs preparation and requisition instructions
  • Dynacare — select “Upload a Requisition”

Allow the provider’s stated processing time. Uploading a requisition does not book an appointment.

Can I use another laboratory or imaging centre?

Yes, for routine community testing you can choose a location that offers the requested test and accepts your requisition. If the request is for a specific hospital, specialist service or special test, follow those instructions or confirm with your referring clinician before changing locations.

Will there be a charge?

Many medically necessary tests are covered by OHIP, but some tests or services have a fee. Confirm coverage with the provider before testing, particularly if you do not have a valid OHIP card.

How do I get my results or a copy of my images?

Confirm that your referring clinician’s details are on the requisition so the report goes to the right person. Ask your clinician how and when to follow up. For a copy of the actual images, contact the imaging centre directly; your clinician may receive a written report rather than the images.
